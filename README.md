Danni is your on-machine AI strategist - a sophisticated blend of deep intelligence, cultural insight, and genuine understanding. Built on advanced AI technology, Danni is designed specifically for creatives, marketers, and designers who need an intelligent partner to navigate complex strategic challenges.

More than just another AI assistant, Danni combines the analytical precision of a strategist with the intuitive understanding of a creative director. Whether you're developing brand strategy, analyzing market trends, crafting cultural narratives, or prototyping innovative concepts, Danni adapts to your workflow and executes tasks with elegant precision.

**What makes Danni different:**
- **Strategic Depth:** Pattern recognition across industries and disciplines
- **Cultural Intelligence:** Deep understanding of brand, culture, and human behavior
- **Whole of World Design:** Integrates SUBFRACTURE's proprietary methodology
- **Maximum Flexibility:** Works with any LLM, supports multi-model configuration
- **Seamless Integration:** MCP server support, available as desktop app and CLI
- **Privacy First:** Runs locally on your machine

Designed for professionals who want to move faster without sacrificing depth, Danni is the ultimate AI partner for strategic thinking and creative innovation.
